Profile

A career built on banking, contract, and commercial litigation

Syed Aijaz Hussain Shirazi has practised law in Karachi since his enrolment as an Advocate of the Subordinate Courts in 2002, progressing to the Sindh High Court in 2004 and the Supreme Court of Pakistan in 2021. His practice spans civil and banking recoveries, contractual and commercial disputes, corporate finance, arbitration, and criminal complaints arising from financial institutions.

Before founding Aijaz Shirazi & Company in 2016, he built his practice as an associate at established Karachi firms — including Ijaz Ahmed and Associates and Mandviwalla & Zafar — conducting trials before District Courts, Banking Courts, and the High Court, and advising financial institutions on syndicate finance, security documentation, and recovery of decretal amounts.

His work is reflected in over twenty reported judgments across Pakistan's law reports, spanning banking recovery, corporate winding-up, arbitration, and constitutional matters.

“Time is a great wealth — our objective is to deliver instant, tailor-made legal service without compromising on rigour.”

Areas of Practice

Full-service counsel for institutions and individuals

Aijaz Shirazi & Company advises financial institutions, companies, private individuals and regulatory bodies, combining courtroom advocacy with commercially-minded advisory work.

Banking & Finance

Loan, security and charge documentation, syndicate facilities, restructuring, legal audits, and recovery proceedings before Banking Courts and High Courts.

Mortgage Finance

Title review, search certificates, mortgage creation and redemption, and registration of mortgage deeds for financial institutions and private borrowers.

Islamic Finance

Shariah-compliant documentation and advisory for Sukuk, musharakah, murabaha and ijara structures.

Arbitration

Drafting and issuing arbitration notices, representation before arbitrators, and challenges to awards before the courts.

Criminal Complaints & Banking Offences

Complaints under the Financial Institutions (Recovery of Finances) Ordinance, pre- and post-arrest bail, and defence before Special Courts (Banking Offences).

Company Law & Business Startup

Incorporation and SECP compliance, drafting of Memoranda and Articles, mergers, winding-up petitions, and shareholder disputes.

Constitutional Matters

Constitutional petitions before the High Courts, including fundamental-rights and public-interest litigation.

Construction & Building

Construction and professional-appointment contracts, environmental NOCs, and dispute resolution for builders and developers.

Family & Guardian Matters

Dissolution of marriage, maintenance, dower recovery, and guardianship and custody proceedings before the Family Courts.

Landlord & Tenant

Rent and lease agreements, fair-rent determination, deposit of rent, and ejectment proceedings before the Rent Controller.

Labour & Service Laws

Advisory and representation before Labour Courts and Service Tribunals for both institutions and individual workers.
